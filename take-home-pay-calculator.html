How this is calculated

This estimate applies progressive national income tax bands — each portion of your income is taxed at its own bracket's rate, so your effective rate is always lower than your top bracket.

This models national income tax only. It does not include state or local tax, social security/National Insurance/EPF contributions, pension deductions, or personal credits and allowances — your actual paycheck will differ. Use this as a directional estimate, not an exact figure.
