How this is calculated

Each month, your contribution is added to the balance, and the whole balance then grows by your expected monthly return. Over many months this compounding effect means growth builds on growth — later years typically add far more than early ones, even with the same contribution.

Actual returns vary and are never guaranteed — this calculator assumes a constant rate for simplicity, while real markets fluctuate year to year.
