How this is calculated

Your payment is calculated using the standard amortization formula: it spreads the loan amount and interest evenly across every month of the term, so each payment is the same size — though the split between principal and interest shifts over time. Early payments are mostly interest; later ones are mostly principal.

Paying extra each month goes straight to your principal, which is why it saves more in interest than the extra amount itself — every dollar off the principal is a dollar that stops accruing interest for the rest of the term.
